News Intel

Privacy

Last updated 30 October 2026

News Intel is a news briefing built by Presolv360. It shows tech, legal and business news gathered from public news feeds. This page explains what it keeps about you when you use it.

What we keep

  • Your account: your name, your email address and your role (member or admin). If you sign in with Google, Google shares only your name, email address and profile picture link with us. We never see your Google password.
  • What you create: your notes, the stories you save, your settings (such as the domains you follow) and any source you ask us to add.
  • A short activity log of sign-ins and admin changes, so admins can see who changed what.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or share your information, and we don't show ads.
  • We don't use analytics or tracking scripts.
  • Other members can't see your notes, saves or settings. Admins can see the list of members and their email addresses, to manage access.

Where it is stored

Accounts and your data are stored with Supabase, our database provider. Your browser also keeps a copy of your saves and notes so the site works offline. Signing out ends your session on that browser.

Deleting your data

To delete your account and everything you created, email aleem.rayeen@presolv360.com from the address you signed up with. We delete it within 30 days.
